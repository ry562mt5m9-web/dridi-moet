# Part 3 – HTML + CSS Together (Absolute Beginner)

**Setup:** For each exercise create two files in the same folder: `index.html` and `style.css`.
Link them with `<link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">` inside `<head>`.

Solutions are in `04-corrections.md`.

---

## Exercise 1 – Styled business card
HTML: a `div` with your name (`h1`), your job (`p`), and your email (`p`).
CSS: center the card on the page, give it a border, padding, a background color and rounded corners.

## Exercise 2 – A simple navigation bar
HTML: a `<nav>` with a list of 4 links (Home, About, Services, Contact).
CSS: remove the bullets, display the links in a horizontal row, give the bar a dark background with white text, and change the link color on hover.

## Exercise 3 – Photo gallery
HTML: 3 images inside a `<div class="gallery">` (use any pictures).
CSS: show the images side by side (flexbox), same width (200px), with a gap, rounded corners, and a border.

## Exercise 4 – Styled table
HTML: a table with 4 rows and 3 columns (Product, Price, Stock).
CSS: collapse the borders, style the header row (colored background, white text), add padding, and use alternate row colors (`tr:nth-child(even)`).

## Exercise 5 – Contact form
HTML: a form with name, email, message (`textarea`) and a submit button.
CSS: make the fields full width, add spacing, rounded borders, and a colored button that changes color on hover.

## Exercise 6 – Final project: A personal landing page
Build a page with:
- A `<header>` with your name and a navigation bar
- A hero section with a big title and a short text, with a colored background
- An "About" section with a photo and a paragraph side by side
- A "Skills" section with a list displayed as small colored badges
- A `<footer>` with a copyright text

Use at least: classes, flexbox, colors, padding/margin, hover effects.
